# Look Here Please: brand proposal (Opus, 2026-11-17)

An independent proposal. Nothing in production is changed. Everything here is
generated by `build.sh` from one geometry in `build.py`.

Start with `exports/before-after.png`, then `exports/icon-review.png`.

## What is weak today

1. **The gesture misses.** The finger in `assets/AppIcon.icon` points about 30°
   left of the attention dot, so the icon never lands its own joke. "Point it
   out" needs the fingertip aimed at the thing.
2. **The thumb reads as a second finger.** The raised thumb beside the index
   makes a V or rabbit-ear silhouette at 32 and 64 px. In the banner it comes
   close to an impolite gesture.
3. **The cuff reads as a shadow.** The thin plum slab sits behind the glove and
   looks like a dropped shadow or a book, not a cuff.
4. **Small composition.** The glove covers about 40% of the tile, and the dot
   (amber on coral, low contrast) is a speck below 64 px.
5. **The icon doesn't match the app.** Every pointer in the app is a glossy toy
   hand, and the signature one (`assets/hand_glove.png`) is a glove on a black
   stick. The icon uses a generic flat hand, so the product's best visual idea
   is missing from its identity.
6. **No wordmark.** The name is set in tracked caps Helvetica. The site and
   README never show the hand collection, which is the most charming thing the
   app has.
7. **Inconsistent name.** The repo is `please-look-here`, the folder is
   `Please Look Here`, and the product is `Look Here Please`.

## What to keep

- The coral, cream and plum palette. It's warm and stands out among blue Mac
  utilities.
- "Point it out." as the line.
- The attention dot as the "here".
- The Icon Composer layered source, template menu bar art with on/off state,
  and the honest local-review and TinyHand credit language.
- The earlier stick idea from `brand-refresh/vector` v3 and concepts A-1 and C-1.
  It was right. Those executions had a stubby mitten finger and no aim.

## Direction: "The pointer lands"

One object and one axis. A cream toy glove on a plum stick runs on a single 45°
diagonal from the lower-left corner, and the fingertip touches an amber target
with a tap ring. The stick, finger and target are collinear, so the gesture
always lands on the target.

| Element | Decision |
|---|---|
| Glove | Original geometry: long round-tipped index, squared knuckle line, three curled-finger rolls on the palm side, separate cuff. No thumb shows, so no V silhouette. Negative-space seams (14/1024) separate the parts and need no outline color. |
| Stick | Plum (light) or coral (dark). Bleeds off the lower-left corner, the way `hand_glove.png` looks in use. |
| Target | Amber dot plus a 60%-opacity ring: a tap or click ripple. Wi-Fi-style arcs were tried and dropped because they read as wireless. |
| Icon Composer | 4 layers: Ping, Target, Glove, Stick. Coral automatic gradient, plum in dark mode. In tinted mode the stick and ping drop to reduced opacity so the glove leads. Compiles with `actool`; all 6 renditions are exported with `ictool`. |
| Menu bar | A separate heavier drawing for 18 pt: solid fist with scalloped knuckles (touching capsules, no interior seams), cuff, no stick. The target is filled when on and hollow when off. The current glyph's thumb stub also goes. |
| Wordmark | **Look Here Please**, with "Here" in coral. Stacked lockup: the glove points straight at "Here", so the name does what it says. Horizontal lockup: tile + one line. Set in live system rounded type (`ui-rounded` on the web, `.fontDesign(.rounded)` + heavy in SwiftUI). |
| Line | "Point it out." The site H1 ends in an amber target dot instead of a period. |

### Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Signal Coral | `#E8452B` (deep `#C9321F` for gradient foot) | Icon field, "Here", accents. Large text only on cream (4.3:1) |
| Glove Cream | `#FFF4DF` | Glove, light page background |
| Plum Ink | `#3D2134` | Text, stick. 13:1 on cream |
| Dusk Plum | `#24151F` | Dark page and dark tile |
| Target Amber | `#FFC94D` | The dot only. Never text |

These are small shifts from s024 (`#E64029`, `#FFF2D6`, `#47293D`, `#FFCC75`).
The amber moves toward yellow so it separates from the coral. The rest could
stay at the s024 values with no visible loss.

## Review concerns for the parent

- **16 px is thinner than s024.** A diagonal glove plus stick has less mass
  than the old blobby hand. It reads as a diagonal pointer and dot rather than a
  hand. The seams go to mush at 16 to 32 px. Icon Composer can't take
  per-size art, so this is the trade for a gesture that lands. Check
  `icon-review.png` at 16 and 32 before adopting.
- **The menu bar glyph has only been checked in renders.** It hasn't been tried
  in the real menu bar next to other items. It's heavier than the current one and
  the off ring is 1.6 px at @2x.
- **Fonts.** The comps rasterize the system rounded face. That's fine as live
  text on the web and in SwiftUI. Outlined or static marketing art (banner PNG,
  og image) made from SF fonts is a licensing gray area. For outlined logos, use
  an OFL rounded grotesk or redraw the 15 letters.
- **The amber ring is at 60% opacity on coral** and goes orange in Default. That's
  intended, but it's the weakest-contrast element.
- **Tinted and clear** treatments come from Icon Composer. The cream seams
  disappear in those looks, and the silhouette carries the icon there.
- **Name order** (point 3 above) is Austin's call, not a design call.
- **Not done:** the og image, Stream Deck category art, a `make-*.py`
  integration, an app build or a real menu bar test. The headless-Chrome
  screenshots follow the system's dark mode. The light render forced light
  colors on a temporary copy that was deleted afterwards.
