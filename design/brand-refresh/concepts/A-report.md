## Recommendation and limitations

A-1 is the strongest overall because the full toy object, short black handle, and cream pointing silhouette remain clear at 32 px, and the coral preserves the product's warm tone.

A-2's tiny dot loses most of its glow at 32 px. A-4's dark stick has less contrast on plum. Fine sculpting on all four gloves simplifies at 32 px. A-3 intentionally crops the stick off the lower-left corner. No generations were rejected or regenerated. No malformed extra fingers, stray text, or baked-in rounded tiles were observed. No git commands, builds, or tests were run. Only A-prefixed files were written in the repo; the image tool also wrote its default generated originals outside the repo, and review thumbnails were written in /tmp as requested.
