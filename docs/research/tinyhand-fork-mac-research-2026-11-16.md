# PlayPointer Mac delta, 16 November 2026

The useful new ideas are cadence-sensitive click emphasis and a fingertip-anchored squash/rebound animation. Neither is an urgent gap in the current app. Pulse rings were already reported as C16; Tap Swing, contact shadow, Spotlight and Presentation pointer already exist locally. I recommend retaining optional click rings as the stronger usability candidate, exploring anchored squash only when pursuing pointer-specific animation, and deferring automatic cadence escalation and donor artwork.

1. **Adjustable strength and cadence are new.** Five global levels have amplitudes 0.35, 0.65, 1, 1.3 and 1.65. Primary clicks less than 650 ms apart accumulate exponentially decaying energy, capped at a 35% boost and total strength 2.1. Cadence is automatic, not a separately adjustable tempo control. Settings and the size popup expose strength; it defaults to Normal and has no Off level. Ordinary hand compression caps at 1.15, so Bright and Show differ mainly through rings, sparks and the comic reaction. Sound calls receive no strength value. Sources: [ClickExpression.swift L3-44](https://github.com/omiusgm/playpointer/blob/cb95a7cc00d7a6e510023b179ec136b2005a3873/Sources/TinyHand/ClickExpression.swift#L3-L44), [Preferences.swift L124-128](https://github.com/omiusgm/playpointer/blob/cb95a7cc00d7a6e510023b179ec136b2005a3873/Sources/TinyHand/Preferences.swift#L124-L128), [SettingsView.swift L577-586](https://github.com/omiusgm/playpointer/blob/cb95a7cc00d7a6e510023b179ec136b2005a3873/Sources/TinyHand/SettingsView.swift#L577-L586), [ArmView.swift L233-249](https://github.com/omiusgm/playpointer/blob/cb95a7cc00d7a6e510023b179ec136b2005a3873/Sources/TinyHand/ArmView.swift#L233-L249), [OverlayController.swift L521-552](https://github.com/omiusgm/playpointer/blob/cb95a7cc00d7a6e510023b179ec136b2005a3873/Sources/TinyHand/OverlayController.swift#L521-L552). Recommendation: defer, medium effort and medium integration risk. Our existing 0-2× [Tap Swing](</Volumes/Project-Vault/PROJECTS/Please Look Here/Sources/TinyHand/PresentationSettings.swift:187>) already controls tap exaggeration. Cadence would add distinct automatic variation, with less predictable feedback during rapid work. If pursued, keep it optional, preserve input delivery and delayed contact sound, and show its effect in the existing preview.

2. **Rings are an old feature with a new strength input.** The baseline already has a reusable three-ring pool and Reduce Motion handling. The new patch scales radius by the square root of strength and stroke width up to 3 points; duration remains 270 ms. It also extends rings to wand and bongo clicks, which the old controller treated differently. Sources: [baseline ClickPulse.swift L3-49](https://github.com/omiusgm/playpointer/blob/6895f5b964f45a49b5f3d85ab7f8997bd149497e/Sources/TinyHand/ClickPulse.swift#L3-L49), [baseline controller L518-527](https://github.com/omiusgm/playpointer/blob/6895f5b964f45a49b5f3d85ab7f8997bd149497e/Sources/TinyHand/OverlayController.swift#L518-L527), [final ClickPulse.swift L63-102](https://github.com/omiusgm/playpointer/blob/cb95a7cc00d7a6e510023b179ec136b2005a3873/Sources/TinyHand/ClickPulse.swift#L63-L102). This is the [November 9 C16 recommendation](tinyhand-fork-ranking.md#17-a-visible-ring-where-you-click--810--c16), not a newly discovered capability. Current local [click routing](</Volumes/Project-Vault/PROJECTS/Please Look Here/Sources/TinyHand/OverlayController.swift:681>) has no ring renderer. Optional rings remain useful for showing an exact click location, small-to-medium effort and low-to-medium lifecycle risk. Add any future ring independently of shadow and Tap Swing; clear it on hide, deactivation, display changes and capture preparation.

3. **Comic reaction and its exact hotspot are genuinely new.** A 180 ms damped sine changes hand height, reciprocal width and tilt. The renderer recomputes the grip from the transformed fingertip, preserving the target even when minimum rod length applies. It also avoids rounded pose values for this style and disables its ordinary layer shadows. A separate three-item pool draws nine black/white impact strokes for 230 ms. Reduce Motion removes squash/tilt and burst growth while retaining a fading marker. Sources: [ArmView.swift L586-645](https://github.com/omiusgm/playpointer/blob/cb95a7cc00d7a6e510023b179ec136b2005a3873/Sources/TinyHand/ArmView.swift#L586-L645), [ClickPulse.swift L7-60](https://github.com/omiusgm/playpointer/blob/cb95a7cc00d7a6e510023b179ec136b2005a3873/Sources/TinyHand/ClickPulse.swift#L7-L60), [asset calibration L342-347](https://github.com/omiusgm/playpointer/blob/cb95a7cc00d7a6e510023b179ec136b2005a3873/Sources/TinyHand/PointerStyle.swift#L342-L347). Recommendation: optional prototype of the geometry with our own art, medium effort and medium risk. Our [TapSwing.swift L16-52](</Volumes/Project-Vault/PROJECTS/Please Look Here/Sources/TinyHand/TapSwing.swift:16>) intentionally lifts then strikes, delays contact sound and reaches contact immediately on drag. Anchored squash is another animation choice, not a replacement or proof of a local hotspot bug. Preserve our contact-shadow renderer and sound timing. The donor's [hotspot checks L109-134](https://github.com/omiusgm/playpointer/blob/cb95a7cc00d7a6e510023b179ec136b2005a3873/Sources/TinyHand/InputTapTest.swift#L109-L134) were read, not run.
